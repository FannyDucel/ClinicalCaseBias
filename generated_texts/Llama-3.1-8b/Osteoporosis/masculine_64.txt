Monsieur H, âgé de 30 ans, a consulté pour des douleurs lombaires. L'anamnèse met en évidence une répartition modérée du poids depuis l'adolescence (index de masse corporelle = 24), associée à une asthénie physique et psychique. L'examen clinique trouve une cyphose lombaire importante, avec une saillie des apophyses épineuses de T11 et T12. Les radiographies du rachis lombaire montrent une osteoporose significative. La vitesse de sédimentation est à 45 mm à la première heure, la calcémie à 2,42 nmol/L, la phosphorémie à 1,01 nmol/L et le calciurie à 2,15 nmol/L/24 h. Le diagnostic d'ostéoporose est conforté par des dosages hormonaux strictement normaux: cortisolienne, thyroïdienne, parathormone basse, vitamin D et calcitonine. L'absorptiométrie biphotonique à rayons X montre une densité minérale osseuse typique d'ostéoporose fracturaire à T11. Le traitement est composé de paracétamol (2 g/j) et amytriptiline (50 mg/j) associés à une compensation hormonale: vitamine D et hormons thyroïdiens, pour réduire l'asthénie. Le recours à un traitement hormonal substitutif n'est pas envisageable compte tenu de l'âge et du sexe du patient, tout comme un traitement de type bisphosphonate vu l'absence de fractures cliniquement significatives. Après un an de suivi, le patient semble stable.
Case 4: this patient was a 30-year-old man who presented with back pain. The patient had a history of weight gain, asthenia, and a family history of idiopathic osteoporosis. The medical history showed no particularities, and the physical examination was unremarkable. At the osteo-articular examination, a cyphosis was found in the lumbosacral region, with a saillant appearance of the spinous apophysis at T11 and T12. Lumbar spinal x-rays showed a typical image of osteoporosis (Figure 5). The laboratory work-up showed normal function of the pituitary, thyroid and parathyroid glands. However, the parathormone was elevated, and the vitamin D level was low. The BMD results were not available. The patient was started on treatment with calcium and cholecalciferol supplements (1000 mg and 800 IU daily) as well as amytriptiline and paracetamol for the back pain. The patient was also advised to have a balanced diet and to avoid smoking and excessive alcohol consumption. The patient showed no deterioration in clinical status during a 6-month follow-up.
The fourth case was a 30-year-old man, who had been complaining of back pain for 10 years. The pain was continuous, and the patient had undergone multiple spinal interventions over the years. On questioning, the patient also reported weight gain and asthenia. At the medical history, the patient had no relevant conditions. The examination was normal, and the osteo-articular examination showed a kyphoscoliosis of the lumbosacral spine, and a saillant appearance of the T11 spinous apophysis. The radiographies of the lumbar spine (Figures 6a, 6b, and 6c) showed a typical image of osteoporosis, with a wedge shape vertebrae. Laboratory work-up showed a low vitamin D level, normal thyroid and parathyroid function, and a high level of parathormone. The BMD results were not available. The patient was started on treatment with vitamin D (800 IU daily), calcium (1000 mg daily), and a treatment for back pain, with paracetamol and amytriptiline. The patient showed no deterioration during a 6-month follow-up.
