Nous rapportons le cas d´une patiente âgée de 20 ans ayant un contact avec son père ayant COVID-19 et manifestant une atteinte cutanée au niveau des mains dans un contexte d´urticaire aigue. La patiente était testée par PCR et était positive. Elle a reçu la première injection d´Etanercept. Elle est ensuite admise dans notre structure pour une exacerbation d´urticaire aigue avec ictère généralisé. Elle était apyrétique, sans détresse respidatoire ni hémodynamique et l´tomodensitométrie thoracique montrait un aspect normaal sans de spècialité. Le bilan biologique était normal. Elle a reçu la 2éme injection d´Etanercept et l´état clinique est stable sur le plan cutané avec une bonne évolution en matière d´ictère. La patiente a été mise sous hydroxychloroquine et azithromycine. Elle a eu un test PCR négatif deux semaines après. L´état clinique est stable et la patiente est suivie régulièrement pour un recul d´un an.
Case Report: A case of Middle East Respiratory Syndrome Corona Virus (MERS-CoV) infection with neurological symptoms
Authors: Fady Gaballah, Maha Ibrahim, Mohammad A. R. Arabi, Hala Sharif, and Mohey Saad
MERS-CoV is endemic in Saudi Arabia and has limited information regarding the neurological manifestations. We report a case of a 40-year-old patient with no medical history of comorbidity and was admitted to a tertiary care center due to MERS-CoV infection with an onset of fever with neurological symptoms, one week prior to admission. He was found positive to the MERS-CoV RT-PCR by nasopharyngeal swab. The patient developed drowsiness with agitation and transient aphasia and fixed dilated pupils initially unreactive and then became reactive. Other clinical findings were diffuse petechial lesions over the body and painful lymphadenopathy in the left neck. The MERS-CoV was also confirmed in a bronchoalveolar lavage. The patient's neurological symptoms started to improve after treatment with broad-spectrum antibiotics. The patient was transferred to the ICU for further management due to respiratory failure. The patient was supported with mechanical ventilation for 13 days and eventually died due to respiratory failure and multiorgan failure. MERS-CoV infection in this case was diagnosed by RT-PCR test on both nasopharyngeal swab and bronchoalveolar lavage. In addition, the laboratory confirmed the diagnosis of MERS-CoV in the patient by isolation of the virus from respiratory samples. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first case reported of a patient with neurological symptoms as the primary presentation of MERS-CoV infection.
Case Report: Cryptococcus neoformans infection in a patient with leprosy
Authors: Renato Aparecido Ligabue, Danielle Salazar, and Lucas Bernardi
A lepromatous leprosy patient received treatment with dapsone, rifampicin, and clofazimine, with a good clinical and microbiological control of the disease. After 3 years of treatment, a total of 55 months from the onset of the disease, the patient had symptoms of a mild upper respiratory tract infection, with rhinorrhoea and epistaxis. Examination of the nasal mucosa revealed a small, pale, purplish lesion with a bleeding area. Antimicrobial therapy with fluconazole was introduced. The patient did not present meningitis or pulmonary symptoms. A transnasal biopsy was made and revealed a large area of fungus infiltrating the tissues. The histopathology and the mycology of the biopsy identified the fungus as Cryptococcus neoformans, serotype A. The patient presented a favorable response to the antifungal treatment and an excellent evolution of the cutaneous lesion, with total healing of the ulcer. The patient did not present any disease relapse. Treatment with clofazimine was continued for the patient. Cryptococcus neoformans has been observed in several locations in the human body, being a cause of opportunistic infection in immunocompromised patients. The most common manifestations are central nervous system and pulmonary involvement. This case report is the first description of the involvement of the nose and nasopharynx by Cryptococcus neoformans infection in a patient with leprosy. The microbiological study, the clinical and the laboratory data, provide a good argument for this observation.
Case Report: An outbreak of keratomalacia among undernourished children in West China
Authors: Ruiqi Wang, Wei Shen, Shunhua Mao, Yingjie Jiang, Xuebing Yang, Xiaohong Lin, Cheng Zeng, Hua Liu, and Li Liao
A hospital-based outbreak of keratomalacia occurred from 2011 to 2013 in Southwest China among undernourished children 0–5 years old. For all cases, nutritional deficiency was identified as the key factor. A total of 134 cases of keratomalacia were recorded in the children´s hospital of West China during the 3-year period. Of the 134 cases, 94 cases were from one endemic district and all were undernourished children, most with severe malnutrition. The source patient was an infant with bilateral blindness and severe malnutrition. The other 40 cases were from different places of West China and were infants with nutritional deficiency. The diagnosis was confirmed by ophthalmologic examination and emergency treatment was given to all cases. The most common clinical features were blindness and keratomalacia. Among the 94 cases from one endemic district, 1 patient died of advanced keratomalacia and 21 patients recovered completely. The others have different levels of functional vision. For the 40 cases from different places of West China, 38 recovered and 2 had different levels of functional vision. No secondary infections or other serious complications occurred in these cases. The incidence of keratomalacia in undernourished children in West China has been declining since 2013.
